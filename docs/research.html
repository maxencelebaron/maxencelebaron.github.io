Research Motivations & Core Research Interests

My goal is to develop new algorithms for Reinforcement Learning and Sequential decision-making. I am particularly interested in understanding the theoretical properties of learning algorithms and using these insights to guide algorithm design.
In this context it can be useful to study simpler settings before addressing the full complexity of Reinforcement Learning. In particular, bandit problems provide a tractable framework for studying fundamental questions such as convergence, regret guarantees, stability, and learning dynamics. The goal is then to determine which of these insights can be transferred to more general Markov decision processes and used to develop new Reinforcement Learning algorithms.
My background in Machine Learning also motivates my interest in Deep Reinforcement Learning. More broadly, I am interested in Deep Learning, particularly its theory and the design of new architectures for different learning tasks, both within and beyond Sequential decision-making.

Broader Research Interests

  • Stochastic Calculus and Bayesian statistics.
    My background in statistics led me to develop a strong interest in probability and stochastic processes. This naturally connects to Stochastic Calculus and MCMC, which also provide useful tools for optimization and the analysis of learning algorithms. I am also interested in Bayesian methods for uncertainty quantification and sequential decision-making.
  • Time Series and Graphs.
    I am particularly interested in learning problems involving data with specific underlying structures. My interest in time series developed during my studies at ENSAI, particularly through coursework in economics and finance. I am interested in online learning for sequentially evolving data, beyond classical approaches to time series analysis. I am also interested in machine learning on graphs, particularly in the context of bandit problems, where graph structure can be incorporated into the learning process.
  • Graph Signal Processing.
    I am interested in Graph Signal Processing as a framework for analyzing and learning from signals defined over graph-structured data. In particular, I am interested in dynamic graphs, where both the graph structure and the associated signals evolve over time, and in the use of graph-based representations in Reinforcement Learning and Sequential decision-making.
  • Graph & Signal Processing
  • Stochastic Calculus
  • MCMC
  • Bayesian methods
